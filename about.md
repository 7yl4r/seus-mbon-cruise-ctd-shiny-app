# About the SFER MBON Cruises

The South Florida Ecosystem Restoration Research project (SFER) is a collaborative effort between NOAA's [Atlantic Oceanographic and Meteorological Laboratory (AOML)](https://www.aoml.noaa.gov/), the University of South Florida (USF), the [Marine Biodiversity Observation Network (MBON)](https://marinebon.org) and other partners to study changes in coastal ecosystems in southern Florida.
These cruises collect a variety of oceanographic and biological data to better understand the health and dynamics of marine ecosystems in this region.

## Cruise Data Tools
The following tools have been developed to facilitate access to and analysis of data collected during the SE-US MBON cruises:

Tool                   | Description                                             | Link 
---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------
SFER Data Viewer       | View SFER data collected during SE-US MBON cruises.     | https://btroast-work.shinyapps.io/SFER_Data_Viewer/
CTD Processing Reports | View details of CTD cleaning processes.                 | https://usf-imars.github.io/seus-mbon-cruise-ctd-processing/
CTD Data Explorer      | Explore and visualize CTD data from SE-US MBON cruises. | https://7yl4r.shinyapps.io/seus-mbon-cruise-ctd-shiny-app/
